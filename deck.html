Application Protocol CRITICAL GUIDELINE
Initial Proposal vs. Final Report: What Is Required When?
Common Misconception Busted: You do NOT need a completed report to apply. The final report is produced after 9 months of incubation.
Key Takeaway
Do you submit a complete final report when registering? NO. You only submit a preliminary proposal (報名計畫書) outlining problem observations and early ideas. The final comprehensive outcome report (預期成果) is completed and submitted 9 months later for the final gala.
Step 1 • Initial Application Sep 21 – Nov 30
What You Submit at Registration
報名收件階段 • Initial Proposal Only
Teams submit two standard documents (under 10MB each via the online portal):
  • 1. Project Proposal Form (報名計畫書): Initial concept, problem observation, envisioned impact. (Finished proof or prototype is NOT required).
  • 2. Participation Affidavit (參賽切結書): Signed agreement uploaded as PDF.
  • Required Depth: Early observation for Action Proposal; summary of existing pilot records for Practical Outcome.
Initial Evaluation Preliminary Written Review (Dec)
Step 2 • Final Review September (Next Year)
What You Submit at Final Review
決選與成果發表 • Completed Deliverables
After 9 months of workshops, testing, and NT$ 15K–30K action funding, teams deliver:
  • Complete Final Outcome Report (預期成果):
    • Proposal Track: Action Blueprint (行動藍圖) with validated needs & prototype metrics.
    • Outcome Track: Impact Outcome Scheme (影響力成果方案) with evidence of impact & scaling plan.
  • Final Pitch Deck & Live Defense: Presented before external expert jury.
Final Evaluation Final In-Person Jury Defense
The Incubation Bridge: Between application and final review, teams receive dedicated mentors, prototyping support, and direct funding in two installments (1/3 upon initial selection; 2/3 upon milestone completion).

Compliance & Ground Rules CRITICAL CAUTIONS FOR APPLICANTS
Crucial Cautions: 6 Traps Every Participating Student Must Avoid
Essential administrative, financial, and academic integrity rules to safeguard your funding and thesis progress.
Hard Rule 01 • Registration
Registration & Team Rules
報名與團隊組成禁忌
  • Strict 12:00 PM Noon Deadline: Application closes Nov 30 at 12:00 PM sharp (NOT midnight!). The system locks automatically.
  • 1 Team Representative Limit: You may join multiple teams, but you can only serve as Team Lead (代表) for ONE project.
  • Signed Affidavit Mandatory: All members must sign the Participation Affidavit (參賽切結書) before PDF upload.
  • Roster Locked After Deadline: Team members and track cannot be changed once submitted.
Key Risk Immediate Disqualification
Financial 02 • Funds & Tasks
Funding Accountability
行動金核銷與里程碑約束
  • Phase 2 Fund is Milestone-Gated: The remaining 2/3 (NT$ 10K–20K) is ONLY paid upon passing prototype reviews and submitting Journey Notes.
  • No Receipt Reimbursement (`不另請團隊留單據核銷`): Paid directly to team representative; students are NOT required to submit receipts for reimbursement.
  • Mandatory 1-on-1 Advisories: Teams must attend the two expert consultations; skipping forfeits final jury defense eligibility.
  • No "Ghost Teams": Regular checkpoint participation is audited by coaches.
Key Risk Forfeiture of 2nd Funding
Academic 03 • Thesis Integrity
Academic Thesis Rules
GMBA 學位論文專屬注意事項
  • Team vs. Individual Authorship: Competitions allow 1–5 members, but a Master's Thesis is an individual defense. If in a team, strictly delineate your independent analytical chapters.
  • Early Advisor Agreement: Consult your thesis advisor by December to formally confirm an Applied Thesis / Capstone format.
  • Ethics & Data Compliance: Ensure company data disclosure and user survey privacy conform to university research ethics.
  • Dual-Track Deliverables: Format the final report as an academic thesis simultaneously.
Key Risk Oral Defense Scrutiny
Golden Rule for Students: "Submit proposal by noon on Nov 30, achieve incubation milestones for direct funding, and present your impact with pride." Compliance Checklist
