## 📌 Executive Summary

The **THU IMPACT AWARD** (東海大學未來永續英雄行動者) is an institutional incubation accelerator designed to empower Tunghai University undergraduate, graduate, and **Global MBA (GMBA)** students, faculty, and staff to transform ideas into tangible social and environmental impact.

* **Total Program Funding Pool**: **NT$ 520,000** (NT$ 300,000 Action Funds + NT$ 220,000 Cash Awards)
* **Application Deadline**: **November 30, 2026 at 12:00 PM (Noon)**
* **Incubation Period**: December 2026 – September 2027
