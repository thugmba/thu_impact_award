## 2. Pillar 01: Three Action Themes (三大行動主軸)

Participants are encouraged to ground their proposals in real-world campus or community issues and propose solutions within one of the following three domains:

### Theme 01: Green Coexistence (綠色共生)
*“Toward a Nature-Positive, Sustainable Future”*

* **Description**: Facing climate change, biodiversity loss, and resource depletion, this theme encourages starting from the campus and everyday living environments to rethink relationships between humans and nature, production, and consumption, developing initiatives that balance ecological conservation, resource sustainability, and quality of life.
* **Key Focus Issues**:
  * Climate action, energy conservation, carbon reduction, and net-zero transition
  * Biodiversity, habitat conservation, and environmental education
  * Water resources, clean energy, and environmental governance
  * Circular economy, plastic reduction, and waste repurposing
  * Low-carbon mobility, sustainable food systems, and green lifestyles
* **Illustrative Proposal Ideas**:
  * Revitalizing underutilized campus spaces into dual ecological-educational zones
  * Developing campus plastic reduction, food waste upcycling, or circular resource systems
  * Applying behavioral design to lower energy use across classrooms, dormitories, or offices
  * Establishing citizen-science mechanisms for campus biodiversity surveys and recording

### Theme 02: Inclusive Future (共融未來)
*“Building a Diverse and Inclusive Society”*

* **Description**: Recognizing that every individual possesses unique backgrounds, abilities, and needs, this theme addresses structural barriers in education, daily life, and civic participation. It fosters service innovation, institutional improvement, and cross-group partnerships to build an equitable, welcoming, and empathetic environment.
* **Key Focus Issues**:
  * Educational equity, learning support, and resource accessibility
  * Disability inclusion, barrier-free access, and friendly physical/digital environments
  * Support for elderly populations, youth, underprivileged families, and marginalized groups
  * Mental health, emotional well-being, and social connectedness
  * Gender equality, multicultural diversity, and intergenerational co-creation
* **Illustrative Proposal Ideas**:
  * Redesigning curriculum materials, digital information, or campus services for diverse learning needs
  * Implementing community-support initiatives for seniors, children, or disadvantaged groups
  * Building peer mental health support and social connection networks on campus
  * Hosting intergenerational and cross-cultural initiatives that dismantle stereotypes and foster mutual understanding

### Theme 03: Smart Innovation (智慧創新)
*“Tech-Driven Sustainable Transformation”*

* **Description**: Technology is not merely an efficiency lever—it is a powerful tool to address complex social and environmental challenges. This theme encourages applying data analytics, artificial intelligence, digital applications, and cross-disciplinary skills to deliver innovative, feasible, and public-value-generating sustainability solutions.
* **Key Focus Issues**:
  * AI, data analytics, and digital tool applications
  * Smart campus systems, IoT-enabled living labs, and service workflow optimization
  * Digital governance, open data transparency, and evidence-based decision support
  * Industrial innovation, green transformation, and clean-tech enablement
  * Disaster preparedness, climate resilience governance, and risk early warning
* **Illustrative Proposal Ideas**:
  * Utilizing data analysis to optimize campus energy loads, transit routes, or facility utilization
  * Engineering digital platforms/apps supporting mental wellness, education, or accessibility
  * Establishing unified campus issue reporting, resource coordination, and decision dashboards
  * Implementing sensor networks, computer vision, or automation to monitor and solve environmental hazards

## 3. Pillar 02: Participation & Registration Guidelines (參與方式與報名資訊)

### 3.1 Eligibility & Team Composition
* **Who Can Join**: Open to all Tunghai University faculty, staff, undergraduate, and graduate students. Both solo individuals and team entries are welcome. Cross-departmental, cross-office, and cross-status (faculty-student-staff collaboration) compositions are strongly encouraged.
* **Team Size**: 1 to 5 members per team.
* **External Collaborators**: Outside community partners, industry experts, or organizational representatives may be invited to collaborate. They do *not* count against the 5-member official team limit.
* **Submission Limits**: An individual may participate in multiple team entries, but may only serve as the designated **Team Representative (團隊代表)** for one single submission. The same project may not be submitted to more than one category.
* **Team Representative**: Teams must designate one primary contact person responsible for administrative communication, receiving notices, and coordinating participation.
* **Team Roster Changes**: Team rosters and representatives cannot be altered after the registration deadline. In exceptional circumstances, formal written approval must be requested from the organizers.

### 3.2 Two Application Tracks (報名組別)

Teams choose a track according to the current maturity of their initiative:

* **Still Ideating? (還在想)**: You have identified a problem and hold initial ideas, but have not yet executed ➔ **【Track A: Action Proposal Track】**
* **Already Doing? (已在做)**: You have ongoing actions, service records, or preliminary outcomes ➔ **【Track B: Practical Outcome Track】**

#### Track A: Action Proposal Track (行動提案組)
*“From zero to one: Transforming an idea into structured action”*
* **Target Audience**: Teams that have identified a critical problem and hold an initial concept, but have not yet executed, or still need to refine the problem space and co-design a concrete solution.
* **Prerequisites at Registration (報名時應具備)**: Documented problem observations, initial concepts, and envisioned direction of change. *(No finalized solution or proof required yet).*
* **Core Journey Focus**:
  * **Problem Framing**: Isolating the root cause that needs to be addressed.
  * **Needs Discovery**: Conducting field inquiry into target stakeholders' lived realities.
  * **Prototype Testing**: Building lightweight action prototypes and iterating through user feedback.
  * **Impact Metrics**: Defining strategic goals and early outcome indicators.
* **Expected Final Deliverable (Submitted at Final Review, NOT at registration / 決選時提交)**: An **Action Blueprint (行動藍圖)** encompassing problem definition, target persona, stakeholder mapping, validated needs, prototype findings, and outcome metrics.
* **Maximum Combined Funding & Prize**: Up to **NT$ 65,000** (Action Fund + Awards).

#### Track B: Practical Outcome Track (實踐成果組)
*“Deepening ongoing impact: Validating, systematizing, and scaling”*
* **Target Audience**: Teams that have already launched initiatives and accumulated preliminary results, service records, or partnerships, seeking to consolidate findings and amplify systemic impact.
* **Prerequisites at Registration (報名時應具備)**: Summary of past execution records, preliminary results, and documented field/service experience.
* **Core Journey Focus**:
  * **Goal Realignment**: Re-evaluating the underlying problem and long-term objectives.
  * **Evidence Synthesis**: Curating execution records, qualitative feedback, and empirical data.
  * **Impact Assessment**: Analyzing social, environmental, or institutional change generated.
  * **Scaling Roadmap**: Formulating pathways for sustainability, institutionalization, or replication.
* **Expected Final Deliverable (Submitted at Final Review, NOT at registration / 決選時提交)**: An **Impact Outcome Scheme (影響力成果方案)** encompassing project context, execution trajectory, verified outcome evidence, impact evaluation, and scaling roadmap.
* **Maximum Combined Funding & Prize**: Up to **NT$ 100,000** (Action Fund + Awards).

### 3.3 Key Dates & Registration Method
* **Official Portal (線上報名網址)**: [https://ithu.tw/THUIMPACTAWARD](https://ithu.tw/THUIMPACTAWARD)
* **Application Window**: **September 21, 2026 (Mon) to November 30, 2026 (Mon) at 12:00 PM (Noon)**. Late submissions are not accepted.
* **Submission Procedure**: The Team Representative submits the application online via [https://ithu.tw/THUIMPACTAWARD](https://ithu.tw/THUIMPACTAWARD) with two required documents (maximum 10MB each):
  1. **Project Proposal Form (報名計畫書)**: Completed using the official track template in `.doc`, `.docx`, or `.pdf` format.
  2. **Participation Affidavit & Consent Agreement (參賽切結書)**: Signed by all members and uploaded as a `.pdf`.
* *Confirmation*: Generation of an official Submission Receipt ID confirms completed application.

## 4. Pillar 03: Capacity Building & Support (培力與支持)

The program provides four layers of institutional backing to help teams navigate execution hurdles:

### 4.1 Four Support Mechanisms
1. **Empowerment Courses & Workshops (培力課程)**: Practical training covering Design Thinking, Problem Framing, Agile Prototyping, and Pitch Presentation.
2. **Professional Consultation (專業諮詢)**: Tailored one-on-one advisory sessions with domain experts to troubleshoot roadblocks, review data, and calibrate project trajectory.
3. **Dedicated Mentorship & Coaching (陪跑輔導)**: Ongoing feedback from designated mentors at key project milestones to mature early concepts into viable solutions.
4. **Learning Portfolio Tracking (歷程累積)**: Documenting team progress through periodic "Journey Notes" (旅程札記), progress monitoring, and outcome reviews.

### 4.2 Project Action Fund (專案行動金)
* **Total Program Action Fund Pool**: **NT$ 300,000**
* **Track Allocations**:
  * **Action Proposal Track**: **NT$ 15,000** per selected team
  * **Practical Outcome Track**: **NT$ 30,000** per selected team
* **Two-Phase Disbursement**:
  * **Phase 1 (1/3)**: Disbursed immediately upon passing the preliminary written review.
  * **Phase 2 (2/3)**: Disbursed upon successful completion of required phase milestones.
* **Eligible Expenditures**:
  * Prototype manufacturing, software/hardware testing, and iteration
  * Stakeholder field interviews, surveys, and focus group research
  * On-site workshops, community pilot activities, and field demonstrations
  * Consumables, materials, and essential professional services
  * Outcome documentation, photography/videography, and impact assessment

## 5. Pillar 04: Review & Awards (評選與獎勵)

### 5.1 Evaluation Stages & Scoring Criteria

#### Action Proposal Track (行動提案組)
* **Preliminary Review (Written Assessment)**:
  * Identified Problem (看見的問題): **35%**
  * Proposed Approach (嘗試的做法): **35%**
  * Envisioned Impact / Change (想帶來的改變): **30%**
* **Final Review (Pitch Presentation & In-Person Jury)**:
  * Issue Insight & Focus (議題洞察與聚焦): **25%**
  * Stakeholder Understanding (利害關係人理解): **20%**
  * Preliminary Validation & Iteration (初步驗證與調整): **30%**
  * Anticipated Change (預期改變): **20%**
  * Presentation & Q&A Defense (簡報與問答): **5%**

#### Practical Outcome Track (實踐成果組)
* **Preliminary Review (Written Assessment)**:
  * Identified Problem (看見的問題): **15%**
  * Stakeholder Identification & Engagement (利害關係人辨識與參與): **10%**
  * Implementation Content & Execution (執行內容): **25%**
  * Outcomes & Generated Change (成果與改變): **25%**
  * Discoveries & Adaptive Iterations (行動中的發現與調整): **15%**
  * Future Direction & Continuity (繼續前進的方向): **10%**
* **Final Review (Pitch Presentation & In-Person Jury)**:
  * Issue Insight & Focus (議題洞察與聚焦): **20%**
  * Stakeholder Engagement (利害關係人參與): **20%**
  * Implementation Quality (執行內容): **25%**
  * Verified Outcomes & Social Impact (成果與影響): **30%**
  * Presentation & Q&A Defense (簡報與問答): **5%**

## 9. Crucial Cautions & Ground Rules (참가자 6대 필수 유의사항)

1. **마감 시간 엄수 (Strict 12:00 PM Deadline)**: 2026년 11월 30일(월) **낮 12:00 정각 마감** (밤 12시 아님!). 포털 자동 차단되며 1분 지각도 접수 불가.
2. **팀 대표 1인 1팀 제한 (Single Lead Rule)**: 팀원으로 여러 팀 참여는 가능하나, **공식 팀 대표(代表)는 오직 1개 팀에서만** 가능.
3. **참가서약서(參賽切結書) 전원 서명 필수**: 팀원 전원 서명된 PDF 업로드 필수.
4. **마감 후 팀 명단 수정 불가**: 11월 30일 접수 마감 후 팀원 추가/제외 및 트랙 변경 일체 불가.
5. **2차 지원금(2/3 잔금) 마일스톤 연동**: 중간 여정 일지 및 프로토타입 참여를 완료해야 잔금(NT$ 10,000~20,000) 지급. 영수증 증빙 필수.
6. **GMBA 개인 논문과 팀 프로젝트 분리**: 대회는 1~5인 팀이지만, 학위 논문은 개인 단독 저작물이어야 하므로 본인의 고유 분석/설계 기여 챕터를 명확히 분리하여 작성.
