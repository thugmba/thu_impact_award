Pillar 01 • Domains

Three Action Themes Grounded in Campus Realities

Participants anchor their initiatives in one of three sustainability domains responding to contemporary challenges.

01 Nature & Climate

Green Coexistence

綠色共生 • Toward Nature-Positive Living

Balancing ecological conservation, resource circularity, and campus quality of life.

  • Climate action & net-zero transition
  • Campus biodiversity & habitat conservation
  • Water, renewable energy & clean governance
  • Circular economy, plastic reduction & food waste
Example: Transforming campus green space into an interactive educational bio-habitat.
02 Equity & Society

Inclusive Future

共融未來 • Building an Empathetic Society

Addressing social barriers through service innovation and institutional design.

  • Educational equity & learning accessibility
  • Disability barrier-free environments
  • Elderly, youth & vulnerable population support
  • Campus mental health & peer connection networks
Example: Peer emotional support systems and barrier-free digital course materials.
03 Tech & Governance

Smart Innovation

智慧創新 • Technology-Driven Transition

Leveraging data analytics, AI, and digital tools to deliver public-value solutions.

  • AI, big data & smart software applications
  • Smart campus facilities & service optimization
  • Open data transparency & decision dashboards
  • Disaster resilience & risk early warning
Example: Developing an AI campus shuttle and energy prediction dashboard to cut emissions.

Compliance & Ground Rules

Crucial Cautions: 6 Traps Every Applicant Must Avoid

Essential administrative, financial, and academic integrity rules to safeguard your funding and thesis progress.

Hard Rule 01 • Registration

Registration & Team Rules

報名與團隊組成禁忌
  • Strict 12:00 PM Noon Deadline: Application closes Nov 30 at 12:00 PM sharp (NOT midnight!). The system locks automatically.
  • 1 Team Representative Limit: You may join multiple teams, but you can only serve as Team Lead (代表) for ONE project.
  • Signed Affidavit Mandatory: All members must sign the Participation Affidavit (參賽切結書) before PDF upload.
  • Roster Locked After Deadline: Team members and track cannot be changed once submitted.
Key Risk: Immediate Disqualification
Financial 02 • Funds & Tasks

Funding Accountability

行動金核發與里程碑約束
  • Phase 2 Fund is Milestone-Gated: The remaining 2/3 (NT$ 10K–20K) is ONLY paid upon passing prototype reviews and submitting Journey Notes.
  • No Receipt Reimbursement (`不另請團隊留單據核銷`): Paid directly in full to the team representative; individual receipt submission is not required by the university.
  • Mandatory 1-on-1 Advisories: Teams must attend the two expert consultations; skipping forfeits final jury defense eligibility.
  • No "Ghost Teams": Regular checkpoint participation is audited by coaches.
Key Risk: Forfeiture of 2nd Funding
Academic 03 • Thesis Integrity

Academic Thesis Rules

GMBA 學位論文專屬注意事項
  • Team vs. Individual Authorship: Competitions allow 1–5 members, but a Master's Thesis is an individual defense. Clearly delineate your independent analytical chapters.
  • Early Advisor Agreement: Consult your thesis advisor by December to formally confirm an Applied Thesis / Capstone format.
  • Ethics & Data Compliance: Ensure company data disclosure and user survey privacy conform to university research ethics.
  • Dual-Track Deliverables: Format the final report as an academic thesis simultaneously.
Key Risk: Oral Defense Scrutiny
